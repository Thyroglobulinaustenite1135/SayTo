## 🧠 Pro Tips for Best Accuracy

1. **Speak naturally** — don't over-enunciate; talk like you would to a friend
2. **Use a quiet room** — background noise reduces accuracy
3. **Position your mic** about 2–3 inches from your mouth, off to the side (avoid popping sounds on "P" and "B")
4. **Say punctuation aloud** — try saying "period" or "question mark" at the end of sentences (works in both languages)
5. **Correct as you go** — if the app mishears a word, simply re-click the mic, say theword again slowly, or click the word in the text box and type manually (keyboard input is always available)

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### ❔ Do I need an internet connection?
No. SayTo works 100% offline once installed. The download requires internet, but the app itself never connects to anything.



### ❔ Is this free?
Yes. SayTo is completely free to useand always will be. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no premium tiers.



### ❔ Can I switch between Persian and English mid-sentence?
Technically, you select one language at a time. However, you can quickly switch languages from the dropdown between words—SayTo resets instantly with no delay, so bilingual dictation is very practical.



### ❔ What if my microphone doesn't work?
First, check that your mic is plugged inand not muted. In Windows, right-click the speaker icon in the taskbar, select **"Sound settings"**, and ensure your mic is selected as the default input device. SayTo also shows a live audio level meter—if you see the bar moving when you talk, your mic is working.



### ❔ Can I type with keyboard too?
Absolutely. The text area is a fully functional text editor. You can click anywhere and type manually to add or correct text, even while dictation is on pause.



### ❔ Will my recordings be stored anywhere?
No. Your audio is never saved to disk. It is processed in real-time memory and immediately discarded. The text you dictate stays in the app window unless you choose to save it.



## 🧰 Troubleshooting

### 🔇 SayTo won't start
- Make sure you have **Windows 10 or newer**
- Close other programs that might interfere (especially other voice apps)
- Try right-clicking the SayTo icon and selecting **"Run as administrator"** once

### 🎤 No text appears when speaking
- Check microphone privacy settings: Go to **Windows Settings → Privacy → Microphone** — ensure **"Allow apps to access your microphone"** is **ON**
- Confirm you're pressing the mic button (it turns red when active)
- Speak louder or reposition the microphone

### 🗑️ App freezes or crashes
- Restart the app (close it completely via X and reopen)
- Check that you have at least 500 MB free disk space
- Try uninstalling (via Windows Settings → Apps) and reinstalling fresh
